Short Biography

Dr. William Kipchirchir Samoei Arap Ruto was born on 21 December 1966 in Kamagut, Uasin Gishu County. He studied botany and zoology at the University of Nairobi, later returning to complete a master's degree and a doctorate. He was first elected to Kenya's National Assembly in 1997 and went on to hold several cabinet positions before serving as Kenya's first elected Deputy President from 2013 to 2022. He was sworn in as the fifth President of the Republic of Kenya on 13 September 2022, having campaigned on a "Bottom-Up Economic Transformation Agenda."

Note: Kenya does not observe a national public holiday for the President's birthday, and no official state celebration programme could be verified for this date. This page is an independent, informational tribute — not a record of an official government event.
